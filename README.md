# StudyVerse 📚
### Learn at Your Level. Grow Every Day.

**StudyVerse** is a personalized learning platform designed for CBSE/NCERT students in Classes 6–10. It helps students identify learning gaps, strengthen foundational concepts, practice through adaptive quizzes, clear doubts, and build consistent study habits.

Unlike traditional learning platforms that assume every student understands previous lessons, StudyVerse aims to identify what a student needs to learn next and recommend lessons based on their individual progress.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Provide personalized learning experiences for students.
- Identify subject-wise and topic-wise learning difficulties.
- Help students revisit prerequisite concepts from earlier classes.
- Improve conceptual understanding through explanations and guided practice.
- Create adaptive study plans based on learning performance.
- Encourage consistency through daily study goals and streak rewards.
- Make educational support accessible using free or free-tier technologies.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Personalized Student Onboarding
Students select their class, preferred subjects, and optionally the subjects they find difficult. Students can skip weak-subject selection and complete a combined diagnostic assessment.

### 2. Combined Diagnostic Assessment
Students answer questions across their selected subjects. The assessment records performance by subject and topic to establish an initial learning profile.

When a student answers a multiple-choice question correctly, they can explain their reasoning. This helps distinguish a correct answer from stronger evidence of conceptual understanding. The system should treat this as evidence, not as definitive proof of whether the student guessed.

### 3. Explainable Weakness Detection
StudyVerse analyzes quiz results, repeated mistakes, reasoning evidence, and prerequisite knowledge to identify topics that may need additional practice. Recommendations explain why a topic is suggested.

### 4. Prerequisite-Aware Learning
Students can revisit earlier-class concepts when they struggle with a current topic. For example, a Class 8 student who struggles with algebra can review relevant Class 6 or Class 7 mathematics foundations when supported by the concept map.

### 5. Adaptive Quizzes and Progressive Hints
Quizzes provide topic-specific hints that encourage students to think step by step. Students receive explanations after answering and can practice related questions.

### 6. Adaptive Study Planning
Study recommendations are designed to improve after each completed quiz. Plans prioritize prerequisite gaps, demonstrated difficulties, student preferences, and available study time.
